CALCULATIONS
- Invoice total = base amount + GST. GST fields are amounts already stored by the app, not rates.
- Monthly P&L uses non-draft invoice base amount as sales on invoice date, excluding output GST. It subtracts non-voided purchase, salary, utility, and vehicle costs on their entry dates. If input GST is marked recoverable, exclude it from cost; if it is not eligible/claimed, include it. Operating profit is a management estimate based only on recorded categories, before unrecorded costs.
- FY Report billed totals include GST. Its displayed collected figure counts the full value of invoices whose status is `paid`; partial-payment entries are not added to that display figure. A separate `recordedPartialPayments` value totals dated payment records. Explain which figure is being used when relevant.
- For an invoice with payment records, recorded collected payments equal the sum of those entries; outstanding is max(0, invoice total minus that sum), unless status is paid, in which case distinguish settled status from the detailed payment history. Never count `paidDate` as an extra payment on top of payment entries.
- For date-based payment questions, use only dated payment entries within the requested period. A paid marker without a payment amount is not a dated amount.
- Do not mix GST with base sales in P&L. Do not count voided costs. Show the arithmetic for meaningful totals when useful.
